### *Senior Data Analyst Deliverable - Task 3*

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Approach

In an energy-driven mobile gaming economy with dual currencies (**Mana & Coins**) and exponential inflation ($L^{1.1}$), product success hinges on the **equilibrium between monetization and progression friction**.

If the game is overly stingy (*mana starved*), players encounter an insurmountable wall and churn. Conversely, if the game is overly generous (*hyper-inflation*), currency accumulates rapidly, eliminating any economic incentive to make real-money In-App Purchases (IAP).

This framework establishes a comprehensive analytics taxonomy across **4 Strategic Pillars**:
1. **Pillar 1: Financial & Monetization Health**
2. **Pillar 2: Game Economy Balancing (Sources vs Sinks)**
3. **Pillar 3: Player Progression & Engagement**
4. **Pillar 4: Risk, Security & Payment Reliability**

---

## 2. Metrics Taxonomy & Mathematical Formulations

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        APP METRICS TAXONOMY                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
          │                                 │
   [1. Monetization]                 [2. Game Economy]
   • Gross Revenue (GMV)             • Sink-to-Source Ratio (Rs/s)
   • Net Revenue (70% cut)           • Mana Wastage / Cap-Idle Rate
   • ARPPU & ARPU                    • Realized CPD vs Theoretical
   • Payer Conversion Rate (CR)      • Sinks Allocation Share
          │                                 │
   [3. Progression & Engagement]     [4. Integrity & Fraud]
   • Progression Velocity (t_L)      • Ghost Claim Ratio (Hacker Rate)
   • Progression Friction Index      • Webhook Reconciliation SLA %
   • Session vs Recharge Alignment   • Balance Drift Anomaly Count
```

### PILLAR 1: Financial & Monetization Health

| Metric Name | Business Motivation | Mathematical Formula | Key Business Questions Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Revenue (GMV)** | Measures total fiat currency spent by players on Google Play / Apple App Store before platform commissions. | $\sum \text{amount}$ where `event_type = 'IAP_REVENUE'` | What is the total top-line purchasing volume per day/month? |
| **Net Revenue** | Measures actual cash collected by the company after standard app store platform fees (30%). | $\text{Gross Revenue} \times 0.70$ | What is the net cash flow generated to fund studio operations? |
| **Payer Conversion Rate (CR)** | Tracks the percentage of active players who transition from Free-to-Play to paying customers. | $\frac{\text{Unique Paying Users (UUID)}}{\text{Total Unique Active Users (DAU)}} \times 100\%$ | Are initial starter bundles (e.g. Drops or Bag) converting players effectively? |
| **ARPPU (Avg Revenue Per Paying User)** | Evaluates the spend depth and purchasing power of the paying cohort (*Whale vs Minnow analysis*). | $\frac{\text{Gross Revenue}}{\text{Unique Paying Users}}$ | How much does an active payer spend on average? Is monetization driven by high volume or whales? |
| **ARPU (Avg Revenue Per User)** | Blended monetization productivity across the entire active user base. | $\frac{\text{Gross Revenue}}{\text{Total Active Users (DAU)}}$ | Is user base growth accretive to top-line revenue? |
| **Realized CPD vs Theoretical CPD** | Evaluates whether revenue is generated by low-level or high-level players. If Realized CPD is low, purchases are concentrated among early-game users. | $\frac{\sum \text{Coins Awarded via IAP}}{\sum \text{USD Spent via IAP}}$ | Is the level-based dynamic discount curve (CPD) effectively incentivizing late-game monetization? |

### PILLAR 2: Game Economy Balancing (Sources vs Sinks)

| Metric Name | Business Motivation | Mathematical Formula | Key Business Questions Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sink-to-Source Ratio ($R_{s/s}$)** | **The Core Economy Indicator.** Measures whether the game economy is deflating or inflating.<br>• $R < 0.8$: Hoarding / hyper-inflation (currencies devalued).<br>• $R > 1.1$: Currency starvation (churn risk).<br>• $R \approx 0.90 - 1.05$: Healthy equilibrium. | $\frac{\sum |\text{Amount Spent (Sinks)}|}{\sum \text{Amount Ingested (Sources)}}$ <br>*(Computed separately for MANA and COIN)* | Are currencies accumulating too fast or choking player progression? |
| **Mana Wastage / Cap-Idle Rate** | Measures the percentage of time players remain at max capacity (`balance >= capacity`) without spending. While at cap, auto-recharging stops—wasting potential energy. | $\frac{\text{Total Hours Player Balance is at Capacity}}{\text{Total Registered Player Hours}} \times 100\%$ | Are players disengaging from the game and letting energy sit idle? |
| **Sink Allocation Share** | Tracks the destination of spent Coins: Capacity Upgrades, Speed Upgrades, 2x Boosts, or direct Mana conversions. | $\frac{\sum \text{Coins Spent per Sink Category}}{\text{Total Coins Spent Across All Sinks}} \times 100\%$ | What utility do players value most? (e.g., if 2x Boosts dominate, design new specialized boosters). |

### PILLAR 3: Player Progression & Engagement

| Metric Name | Business Motivation | Mathematical Formula | Key Business Questions Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Progression Velocity ($T_L$)** | Average active time required for a player to complete level $L$ and advance to $L+1$. | $\text{Timestamp}(\text{Level } L+1) - \text{Timestamp}(\text{Level } L)$ | At which level does progression abruptly stall? |
| **Progression Friction Index (PFI)** | Ratio of action cost ($m_a$) to Mana capacity ($C$). If $m_a / C \to 0.5$, a player can only take two actions before waiting for a full recharge. | $\frac{m_a(L)}{\text{Capacity}(L_C)} = \frac{\text{round}(3 \times L^{1.1})}{(3 \times L_C^{1.1}) \times 30}$ | Is the inflation curve penalizing high-level players who do not upgrade capacity? |
| **Session Alignment to Recharge Cycle** | Measures whether players launch the app soon after their Mana reaches full capacity. | Delta between `Timestamp(Cap Full)` and `Timestamp(Next Action)` | How effectively does energy auto-recharge function as a retention hook? |

---

### PILLAR 4: Risk, Security & Payment Health

| Metric Name | Business Motivation | Mathematical Formula | Key Business Questions Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ghost Claim Attempt Rate** | Percentage of client-side purchase callbacks that fail verification against the Payment Gateway. | $\frac{\text{Client Claims Resulting in REJECTED}}{\text{Total Client Purchase Claims}} \times 100\%$ | How prevalent are malicious injection or APK tampering attempts? |
| **Webhook SLA Arrival Rate (< 5 min)** | Percentage of authoritative server-to-server notifications received within 5 minutes of client checkout. | $\frac{\text{Webhooks Received within } \le 5 \text{ Minutes}}{\text{Total Webhook Events}} \times 100\%$ | Are network or payment gateway issues causing delayed coin credits and player frustration? |

## 3. Are These Metrics Suitable for Defining Product/Business Success or Failure?

### Verdict: **YES, HIGHLY SUITABLE.**

However, in gaming analytics, no single metric operates in isolation. Success must be monitored through an interconnected hierarchy of **Leading vs. Lagging Indicators**:

```
           LAGGING INDICATORS (Outcome / Health)
           ▲  • Gross & Net Revenue
           │  • 30-Day Cohort Retention Rate
           │  • Player Lifetime Value (LTV)
           ┼────────────────────────────────────────────────
           │
           │  LEADING INDICATORS (Early Warning Signals)
           ▼  • Sink-to-Source Ratio (Economy Deflation/Inflation)
              • Mana Wastage Rate (Early Churn Predictor)
              • Progression Friction Index at Levels 5–15
```

1. **Definition of SUCCESS (Green Flags):**
   * **Monetization:** Conversion Rate $> 3.5\%$, ARPPU rising steadily as players reach higher levels with higher CPD packages.
   * **Game Economy:** Sink-to-Source ratio stable within the healthy corridor ($0.90 \le R_{s/s} \le 1.05$), indicating active currency circulation without runaway hoarding.
   * **Engagement:** Mana Wastage Rate $< 20\%$, showing that players frequently burn their energy before it idles at capacity.
   * **Integrity:** Ghost Claim Rate $< 1\%$, Webhook SLA arrival $> 99\%$.
2. **Definition of FAILURE (Red Flags):**
   * **Paywall Cliff:** Progression Friction Index spikes sharply (e.g., at Level 8), causing a massive drop-off in retention while conversion rates fail to compensate.
   * **Hoarding Economy:** Sink-to-Source ratio drops below $0.70$. Free gifts and recharge rates outpace content sinks, removing all urgency to buy Coins.
   * **Payment Disruption:** Webhook SLA drops below $90\%$, leading to widespread customer support tickets regarding delayed credits.

## 4. Stakeholder Communication & Dashboard Blueprint

Different stakeholders require targeted presentations of these metrics:

### A. Executive Team (CEO & CFO)
* **Format:** *High-Level Executive KPI Scorecard* (Monthly trendlines & summary cards).
* **Core Focus:** Gross Revenue, Net Revenue, ARPPU, Blended ARPU, Cohort LTV.
* **Key Question:** *"Is the business model profitable, predictable, and scalable?"*

### B. Game Producers & Economy Designers
* **Format:** *Sankey Diagrams (Currency Flow)* & *Level Friction Heatmaps*.
* **Core Focus:** Sink-to-Source ratio by level cohort, Mana Wastage Rate, Coin Sink Allocation (Upgrades vs Boosts vs Mana).
* **Key Question:** *"Where are players hitting friction walls? Are upgrade costs balanced against player earnings?"*

### C. Engineering & Risk Operations
* **Format:** *Real-time Operational Alerting Dashboard (Grafana / Datadog style)*.
* **Core Focus:** Unreconciled Client Claims, Duplicate Webhooks, Webhook SLA Latency, Negative Balance Invariant Breaches.
* **Key Question:** *"Are payment webhooks dropping or are exploit attempts targeting the API?"*

## 5. Actionable Business Playbook

| Observed Anomaly / Metric Shift | Root Cause Diagnosis | Actionable Business & Product Playbook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conversion rate is strong at Levels 1–3, but collapses after Level 10** | Coins Per Dollar (CPD) scaling is insufficient relative to the exponential jump in action costs, making late-game packages feel low-value. | Increase constant $c_3$ from 4.32 to 5.0 on larger bundles (Chest/Barrel) to give late-game spenders higher purchasing power. |
| **Mana Wastage Rate surges from 15% to 45%** | Players lack immediate goals. Energy sits at capacity because sessions are infrequent. | Deploy intelligent push notifications when Mana hits 90%: *"Your energy is full! Complete your adventure now!"*, or introduce time-limited weekend events. |
| **Coin Sink-to-Source Ratio drops below 0.60** | Excessive free gifts are flooding the player base, cannibalizing IAP monetization. | Moderating free coin frequency or introducing cosmetic sinks (skins, titles, aesthetic customization) that drain coins without breaking progression balance. |
| **Ghost Claim Rate exceeds 10%** | Coordinated APK patching or refund abuse tutorial circulating in the player community. | Enforce stricter cryptographic signature validation on API gateways and implement automatic device fingerprint bans on repeated unverified claims. |
